Setting up your Google account correctly from the start saves time and prevents permission headaches later. This page walks you through signing up, personalizing your profile, creating or joining an organization, and bringing your teammates on board.

## Main Setup Flow

<Steps>

  ### Create Your Account

  Go to [app.google.com](https://app.google.com) and choose the sign-up method that works best for you.

  <Tabs>
    <Tab title="Email & Password">
      1. Click **Sign Up** on the landing page.
      2. Enter your **work email address** and choose a strong password (at least 12 characters).
      3. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link.
      4. You're redirected to the onboarding flow to complete your profile.
    </Tab>
    <Tab title="Single Sign-On (SSO)">
      1. Click **Continue with SSO** on the landing page.
      2. Enter your organization's **SSO domain** (for example, `yourcompany.com`).
      3. You're redirected to your identity provider (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, etc.) to authenticate.
      4. On first login, Google automatically provisions your account using the attributes from your identity provider.

      <Note>
        SSO must be configured by an Owner or Admin before individual members can use it. See [Authentication](/configuration/authentication) for setup instructions.
      </Note>
    </Tab>
  </Tabs>

  ### Complete Your Profile

  After signing in for the first time, Google prompts you to complete your profile. You can also update these details at any time by navigating to **Settings → Profile**.

  Fill in the following fields:

  - **Full Name** — used in comments, notifications, and audit logs so your teammates can identify you.
  - **Avatar** — upload a photo or choose a generated avatar. Accepted formats: PNG, JPG, WebP (max 2 MB).
  - **Timezone** — Google uses your timezone to localize timestamps and scheduled notifications. Select the timezone that matches your primary working location.

  Click **Save Profile** when you're done.

  ### Create or Join an Organization

  Organizations are the top-level container for all your workspaces, projects, and team members.

  <Tabs>
    <Tab title="Create a New Organization">
      1. From the dashboard, click **Create Organization**.
      2. Enter an **Organization Name** (this appears in URLs and notifications, so keep it concise).
      3. Optionally upload an organization logo.
      4. Click **Create** — you are automatically assigned the **Owner** role.
    </Tab>
    <Tab title="Join an Existing Organization">
      If a teammate has already set up your organization, ask an Owner or Admin to send you an invitation. Once you receive the invite email, click **Accept Invitation** and you'll be added automatically with the role they assigned.

      <Tip>
        If your organization uses SSO with automatic provisioning, you may be added to the organization the first time you log in — no manual invite needed.
      </Tip>
    </Tab>
  </Tabs>

  ### Invite Team Members

  Once your organization is ready, add your colleagues so they can collaborate on the same projects and resources.

  1. Navigate to **Settings → Team** in the sidebar.
  2. Click **Invite** in the top-right corner.
  3. Enter one or more **email addresses**, separated by commas.
  4. Select a **role** for each invitee (see the [Roles Overview](#roles-overview) table below).
  5. Click **Send Invitations**.

  Each invitee receives an email with a link to accept the invitation. Pending invitations appear in the **Team** list with an **Invited** badge until accepted.

  <Info>
    Invitations expire after **7 days**. If a teammate misses the window, navigate to **Settings → Team**, find the pending invite, and click **Resend**.
  </Info>

</Steps>

## Roles Overview

Google uses role-based access control (RBAC) to manage what each team member can see and do within your organization. Assign the most restrictive role that still meets each person's needs.

| Role       | Description                                                                                     | Typical Use Case                              |
|------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| **Owner**  | Full control over the organization, including billing, SSO configuration, and member management. Owners can delete the organization. | Founders, engineering leads, account administrators |
| **Admin**  | Can manage members, create and delete workspaces and projects, and configure settings — except billing and SSO. | Team leads, IT administrators                 |
| **Member** | Can create, edit, and delete resources within projects they have access to. Cannot manage members or organization settings. | Developers, content editors, analysts         |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to projects and resources they've been granted access to. Cannot create or modify any data. | Stakeholders, external reviewers, auditors    |

<Warning>
  The **Owner** role should be assigned to at least two people in your organization to avoid being locked out if the primary owner loses access to their account.
</Warning>
