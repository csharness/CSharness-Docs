Good data hygiene keeps Google fast and useful as your knowledge base grows. This page covers the full lifecycle of your data — from organizing new resources into projects and tagging them for discovery, to archiving stale content, bulk importing from a spreadsheet, and exporting structured data for downstream use. It also summarizes Google's data retention policy so you know exactly how long your information is kept.

---

## Organize Resources

Google gives you three complementary layers of organization you can combine to fit your team's workflow.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Projects" icon="folder-open" href="/guides/data-management">
    Top-level containers that group related resources together. Every resource belongs to at least one project. Use projects to separate business units, product areas, or client accounts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Folders" icon="folder" href="/guides/data-management">
    Sub-containers within a project. Folders let you create a nested hierarchy — for example a **Products** project with folders for **Mobile**, **Web**, and **API**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Labels" icon="tag" href="/guides/data-management">
    Freeform, cross-cutting tags you apply to individual resources. Labels are great for themes that cut across projects — like `needs-review`, `customer-facing`, or `Q3-2025`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pinned Views" icon="pin" href="/guides/data-management">
    Save a filtered or sorted list view and pin it to your sidebar. Pinned views aren't organizational containers, but they let you surface specific subsets of resources instantly.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Create a project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New Project">
    In the left sidebar, click **+ New Project**. A dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and describe it">
    Enter a project name (required) and an optional description. Choose an icon and color to make the project visually distinct in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set visibility">
    Choose **Private** (only invited members), **Team** (all members of your organization), or a specific team. You can change this later in project settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Project**. The project appears in your sidebar and is immediately ready to receive resources.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Add and manage labels

Navigate to any resource, click the **Labels** field in the detail panel, and type to search existing labels or create a new one. Labels auto-complete as you type. To manage your organization's label library (rename, merge, or delete labels), go to **Settings → Labels**.

## Archive vs. Delete

Google treats archiving and deletion as distinct operations so you can remove clutter without permanently losing data.

| | Archive | Delete |
|---|---|---|
| **Visibility** | Hidden from all list and search views by default. | Removed immediately from all views. |
| **Recovery** | Restore any time from the Archive. | Recoverable within 30 days from **Settings → Trash**. |
| **After recovery window** | Stays archived until you restore or delete it. | Permanently purged after 30 days. |
| **Use when** | Content is no longer active but may be needed later. | Content is definitively no longer needed. |

<Warning>
  After the 30-day recovery window closes, deleted resources are permanently purged from Google's systems and cannot be recovered. If you are unsure, archive instead of delete.
</Warning>

### Archive a resource

Select the resource in a list view (or open it), click the **⋯** overflow menu, and choose **Archive**. The resource disappears from standard views immediately. To view archived content, open any list view and toggle **Show Archived** in the filter bar.

### Restore or permanently delete from Trash

Go to **Settings → Trash**. Use the **Restore** button next to any item to bring it back to its original location, or click **Delete Permanently** to remove it before the 30-day window expires.

## Bulk Import via CSV

Use the CSV import to migrate existing data into Google — for example, from a spreadsheet or another knowledge-base tool.

<Tip>
  Before building your own CSV, download Google's official import template. It includes all required column headers, data format notes, and example rows. Using the template prevents the most common import errors. Find it in **Settings → Import → Download Template**.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the CSV template">
    Go to **Settings → Import** and click **Download Template**. Open the file in your spreadsheet application of choice. Review the column headers and the example row at the top of the sheet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare your data">
    Populate the template with your data. Required columns are marked with an asterisk in the header row. Keep the following in mind:

    - Dates must be in ISO 8601 format: `YYYY-MM-DD`.
    - Multi-value fields (such as labels) use a pipe delimiter: `label-one|label-two`.
    - Leave optional columns blank — do not delete the column headers.

    Save the completed file as a `.csv` (UTF-8 encoding).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the file">
    Back in **Settings → Import**, click **Upload CSV** and select your file. Google validates the file immediately and shows a preview of the first 10 rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map fields">
    If any column headers differ from the template (for example, if you renamed them), use the **Field Mapping** screen to match each CSV column to the correct Google field. Required fields must be mapped before you can proceed.

    <Note>
      Fields that don't have a matching Google field can be mapped to a custom attribute, or skipped entirely. Skipped fields are not imported.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the import">
    Click **Start Import**. Google processes the rows in the background. When the import is complete, you receive an in-app notification and an email summary showing how many rows succeeded, were skipped, or failed with error details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export Data

You can export all resources or a filtered subset at any time in CSV or JSON format.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the data you want to export">
    Open the list view, project, or saved search that contains the records you need. Apply any filters to narrow the result set before exporting — the export includes exactly what's currently visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Export menu">
    Click the **Export** button in the list toolbar. A dropdown appears with two format options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose CSV or JSON">
    - **CSV** — best for spreadsheets and business users. Exports all visible columns. Multi-value fields are pipe-delimited.
    - **JSON** — best for developers and system integrations. Exports all fields including hidden metadata, nested objects, and relationship IDs.

    <Info>
      Filtered exports respect your current view exactly — including column order and any active filters. If you want a complete dump of all data, clear all filters before exporting.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the file">
    For exports under 1,000 rows, the file downloads immediately in your browser. For larger exports, Google queues the job and emails you a secure, time-limited download link when ready. You can also access completed exports in **Settings → Downloads** for 7 days after generation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Data Retention Policy

Understanding how long Google keeps your data helps you plan archiving and deletion workflows appropriately.

<Accordion title="Active resources">
  Resources remain in Google indefinitely as long as your organization's subscription is active. There is no automatic expiration on active content.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Archived resources">
  Archived resources are retained indefinitely in the archive until you manually restore or delete them. They do not count toward any storage quota.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Deleted resources (Trash)">
  Deleted resources are held in **Settings → Trash** for **30 days**, during which they can be fully restored. After 30 days, they are permanently purged and cannot be recovered.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Exported files">
  Export files generated by Google are available in **Settings → Downloads** for **7 days** after creation, then automatically removed from Google's servers. Download and store your exports externally if you need them beyond that window.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Account and audit data">
  Activity logs and audit trails are retained for **12 months** on standard plans and **36 months** on Enterprise plans. Contact [support](/support/troubleshooting) if you need to request an audit log export.
</Accordion>
