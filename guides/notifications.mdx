Google's notification system keeps you and your team informed without creating noise. You can receive alerts inside the app, in your inbox, or delivered directly to an HTTP endpoint — and you control exactly which events trigger each channel. This page covers individual user preferences, org-wide policies, digest emails, and how to verify your setup with a test notification.

---

## Notification Types

Google delivers notifications through three channels. Each channel can be enabled or disabled independently per event category.

| Channel | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **In-app** | Appears in the notification bell (top-right of the app). Persists until dismissed. | Real-time awareness while you're actively using Google. |
| **Email** | Sent to your account email address. Can be immediate or bundled into a digest. | Staying informed when you're away from the app. |
| **Webhook** | POSTs a JSON payload to a configured endpoint whenever a subscribed event fires. | Feeding events into external systems, Slack bots, or custom dashboards. |

## Enable or Disable Notification Categories

Each user manages their own notification preferences independently. Changes you make here affect only your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notification Settings">
    Click your avatar in the lower-left corner of the sidebar, then select **Settings**. In the settings menu, click **Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review notification categories">
    The page lists every notification category grouped by channel (In-App, Email, Webhook). Each row shows the event name, a brief description, and toggle switches for each channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle categories on or off">
    Flip the toggle for any event-channel combination. Changes save automatically — there is no separate Save button. A brief confirmation banner appears at the bottom of the screen after each change.

    <Tip>
      If you want to mute everything temporarily — for example, during a focus block — click **Pause All Notifications** at the top of the page and set a resume time. All your preferences are preserved and automatically re-enabled when the pause expires.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set Organization-Wide Notification Policies

<Note>
  This section requires **Admin** role. Organization-wide policies override individual user preferences for the affected categories.
</Note>

Admins can enforce minimum notification requirements across the organization — for example, ensuring that all members receive critical security alerts regardless of their personal settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Admin panel">
    Click **Settings**, then click **Organization** in the left menu. Select the **Notification Policies** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or edit a policy">
    Click **New Policy**. Give the policy a name, then select the event categories it applies to. For each category, choose one of three enforcement levels:

    - **Required** — all users receive this notification; the toggle is locked in their personal settings.
    - **Default On** — enabled for all users by default, but individuals can opt out.
    - **Default Off** — disabled by default, but individuals can opt in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Target the policy">
    Apply the policy to the entire organization, a specific team, or users with a particular role. A policy can target multiple groups simultaneously.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the policy">
    Click **Save & Activate**. Google immediately applies the policy to all affected users and records the change in the admin audit log.

    <Warning>
      Setting a category to **Required** will re-enable that notification for users who have previously opted out. Communicate policy changes to your team before activating them.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set Up Digest Emails

Instead of receiving an individual email for every event, you can configure Google to bundle activity into a single daily or weekly summary.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Notification Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Notifications** and scroll to the **Email Digest** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable digest mode">
    Toggle **Enable Email Digest** on. The individual email toggles for non-critical events are automatically switched to digest-only mode, though you can override this per category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose digest frequency">
    Select **Daily** or **Weekly** from the **Send Digest** dropdown. For daily digests, choose your preferred delivery time and timezone. For weekly digests, also choose the day of the week.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and save">
    Click **Send Preview Digest** to receive a sample digest email at your account address right now. Review the format, then click **Save** to confirm your schedule.

    <Info>
      If no qualifying events occurred during a digest period, Google skips that delivery rather than sending an empty email.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Test a Notification

After configuring your preferences, use the built-in test tool to confirm that each channel is working correctly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Notification Settings">
    Go to **Settings → Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the notification category to test">
    Scroll to the category you want to verify — for example **Resource Created** under the **Email** column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Trigger Test">
    Click the **Trigger Test** button (bell icon with a play arrow) next to the category. Google fires a synthetic test event through the selected channel immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm delivery">
    - **In-app:** the notification bell badge increments and the test event appears at the top of the notification drawer.
    - **Email:** check your inbox (and spam folder) within a few minutes.
    - **Webhook:** check your endpoint's request log for a payload with `"event": "test"`.

    <Note>
      Test events are clearly labeled with `"test": true` in webhook payloads and with a **[TEST]** prefix in email subjects so they are easy to distinguish from real events.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Notification Event Reference

The table below lists every event type Google can notify you about, a short description, and whether each channel is enabled by default for new accounts.

| Event | Description | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `resource.created` | A new resource was created in a project you belong to. | In-app: ✅  Email: ✅  Webhook: ❌ |
| `resource.updated` | A resource you own or watch was edited. | In-app: ✅  Email: ❌  Webhook: ❌ |
| `resource.deleted` | A resource was deleted or moved to the archive. | In-app: ✅  Email: ✅  Webhook: ❌ |
| `comment.created` | Someone commented on a resource you own or are mentioned in. | In-app: ✅  Email: ✅  Webhook: ❌ |
| `mention` | You were @-mentioned in a comment or resource body. | In-app: ✅  Email: ✅  Webhook: ❌ |
| `member.invited` | A new member was invited to your organization. | In-app: ❌  Email: ✅  Webhook: ❌ |
| `member.joined` | An invited member accepted and joined. | In-app: ✅  Email: ❌  Webhook: ❌ |
| `permission.changed` | Your role or permissions were updated by an admin. | In-app: ✅  Email: ✅  Webhook: ❌ |
| `export.completed` | A data export you requested has finished and is ready to download. | In-app: ✅  Email: ✅  Webhook: ❌ |
| `integration.error` | A connected integration encountered an error. | In-app: ✅  Email: ✅  Webhook: ✅ |
| `security.alert` | A security event occurred (e.g., new login from unrecognized device). | In-app: ✅  Email: ✅  Webhook: ✅ |
