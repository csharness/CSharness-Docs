Google is built around a handful of high-frequency actions that you'll reach for every day. This page walks through each of those workflows in detail so you can move quickly and confidently — whether you're spinning up a new resource, hunting down a record, or shipping a bulk export to a stakeholder.

---

## Create a New Resource

Every piece of knowledge in Google lives in a **resource**. A resource can be a document, a snippet, a structured record, or any custom type your admin has configured. The creation flow is the same regardless of type.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Resource dialog">
    In the left sidebar, click **New Resource** (or press `N` from any list view). A dialog opens prompting you to choose a resource type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a resource type">
    Select the type that best fits your content — for example **Document**, **Snippet**, or a custom type defined by your organization. The form fields update to match the type you select.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the resource form">
    Complete all required fields (marked with an asterisk). Add an optional description, assign an owner, and attach any relevant labels or tags. Use the rich-text editor for body content.

    <Note>
      Required fields vary by resource type. Contact your admin if you need a field added to a custom type.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the resource">
    Click **Save** to create the resource immediately, or **Save & Open** to land directly on the resource detail page. The new resource appears in your list view and is immediately searchable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Search and Filter Resources

Google's search bar understands plain-language queries and supports structured filter chips so you can narrow thousands of records down to exactly what you need.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the search bar">
    Press `/` from anywhere in the app, or click the search icon in the top navigation. The search bar activates with focus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your query">
    Enter keywords, a resource title, or an owner's name. Results appear in real time as you type. Matches are highlighted in the result list.

    <Tip>
      Wrap a phrase in double quotes to search for an exact match — for example `"onboarding checklist"`.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filter chips">
    Below the search bar, click **+ Add Filter** to add structured filters. Available dimensions include **Type**, **Owner**, **Label**, **Date Created**, and **Status**. You can stack multiple filters — they combine with AND logic by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a search">
    Once you have the filters you want, click **Save Search**, give it a name, and choose whether to make it private or share it with your team. Saved searches appear in the left sidebar under **Saved Searches** for one-click access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit and Update Resources

Google supports two editing modes: **inline editing** for quick field changes and a **full edit modal** for comprehensive updates.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the resource">
    Click any resource in a list or search result to open the detail panel on the right side of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use inline editing for quick updates">
    Hover over any editable field in the detail panel and click the pencil icon that appears. Type your change and press `Enter` or click outside the field to save. No modal required.

    <Info>
      Inline edits are saved immediately and appear in the resource's activity log.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the full edit modal for larger changes">
    Click **Edit** (top-right of the detail panel) to open the full edit modal. This gives you access to all fields, the rich-text body editor, relationship settings, and advanced metadata. Click **Save Changes** when done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the change history">
    Open the **Activity** tab on the resource detail panel to see a timestamped log of every edit, including who made the change and what the previous value was. Click **Restore** next to any entry to revert that field.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Apply Bulk Actions

When you need to update, move, or delete multiple resources at once, bulk actions let you do it in a single operation instead of repeating the same steps on each record.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select multiple resources">
    In any list view, hover over a row and click the checkbox that appears on the left. Repeat for each resource you want to include. To select all visible resources, click the checkbox in the column header.

    <Note>
      Bulk selection is limited to the current page. If you need to act on more records, increase the page size in the table footer first.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the bulk action toolbar">
    As soon as you check at least one row, the bulk action toolbar appears at the bottom of the screen. It shows the count of selected items and a set of available actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose and confirm an action">
    Click the action you want to apply — **Move**, **Label**, **Archive**, **Delete**, or **Export**. A confirmation prompt displays the number of affected resources. Review and click **Confirm** to proceed.

    <Warning>
      The **Delete** bulk action is permanent for resources that are not soft-delete enabled. Verify your selection carefully before confirming.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export Data

You can export any list view — including filtered results — to CSV or JSON at any time. Exports run asynchronously and are delivered to your email or the **Downloads** panel.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the list you want to export">
    Open the resource list, saved search, or filtered view that contains the records you want. Apply any additional filters to narrow the result set before exporting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Export menu">
    Click the **Export** button in the list toolbar (top-right of the list view). A dropdown appears with format options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a format">
    Select **Export as CSV** for spreadsheet-friendly data or **Export as JSON** for structured, machine-readable output. CSV exports include all visible columns; JSON exports include all fields including hidden metadata.

    <Tip>
      If you only need certain columns, use the **Columns** picker (next to Export) to hide unused fields before exporting. The CSV will include only the columns currently visible.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download your file">
    For small result sets (under 1,000 rows), the file downloads immediately. For larger exports, Google queues the job and emails you a download link when it's ready — typically within a few minutes. You can also find completed exports in **Settings → Downloads**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
